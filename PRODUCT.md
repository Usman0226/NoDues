# Product

## Register

product

## Users

University and college personnel managing institutional student clearance workflows:
- **System / College Administrators**: Supervise all academic departments, faculty roster, student directories, master curricula, and department-wide academic rollovers.
- **Heads of Departments (HOD) & Academic Officers (AO)**: Oversee departmental sections, handle due overrides, manage department-level clearance batches, and review faculty actions.
- **Faculty Members (Class Teachers, Subject In-Charges, Mentors)**: Review student clearance requests, approve dues, mark fee/equipment/attendance dues, and audit student trajectory.
- **Students**: Track clearance progression across departments and subjects in real-time, inspect dues, and view final digital clearance certificates.

## Product Purpose

NoDues digitizes and automates the semester and graduation clearance process for higher education institutions. It replaces physical multi-office paper signatures with an atomic, role-based, auditable digital approval system ensuring zero uncollected institutional dues and frictionless graduation clearance.

## Brand Personality

- **Voice & Tone**: Authoritative, Crisp, Trustworthy, Academic Precision.
- **Personality**: Modern Institutional, Frictionless, High-Density Efficiency.
- **Emotional Goals**: Instant confidence for administrators, zero anxiety for students, complete audit clarity for department heads.

## Anti-references

- Outdated legacy university ERPs with bloated tables, micro-fonts, and confusing navigation.
- Monotonous endless card scroll and nested card bloat.
- Uninformative blank states and cryptic error dialogs.
- Loud consumer marketing aesthetics with neon gradients and decorative distraction.

## Design Principles

1. **Earned Familiarity & Task Flow**: Design serves the product. Clean top navigation stack, intuitive tabs for temporal/semester dimensions, and familiar SaaS ergonomics (Linear / Stripe / Notion quality).
2. **Instant Status Hierarchy**: Critical clearance statistics, active batches, and blocking dues must be identifiable within 2 seconds using high-contrast typography and semantic badges.
3. **Fail-Safe & Audit-Ready**: Destructive or cohort-wide actions (promotions, rollovers, overrides) provide preflight previews, strict validations, and transparent confirmation states.
4. **Purposeful Spacing & Rhythm**: Varied, tight grouping for related records (8–12px) paired with generous breathing room (32–48px) between page sections; no nested cards or arbitrary gaps.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target: WCAG 2.1 Level AA compliance.
- Contrast ratio ≥ 4.5:1 for body and data text against light backgrounds.
- High-contrast badges for statuses (cleared: emerald, pending: amber, due: red, override: indigo).
- Accessible touch targets (≥ 44×44px hit areas) and screen-reader labels with native tooltip titles on truncated headers.
- Motion matches user preference via `prefers-reduced-motion`.
